Generalized Q&A from an anonymized sales conversation; not a new price or policy list.
Q: Can you make my jacket design?
A: Send the design, quantity, sizes, fabric, colors and logo placements so we can confirm feasibility and inclusions.
Q: Can I see a sample first?
A: We can discuss an actual sample for approval. Confirm the current sample fee, any credit toward the order and turnaround; past arrangements are not standard offers.
Q: What logo file is needed?
A: Please send a high-resolution logo so embroidery details are clear.
Q: Can you revise fit, fabric or embroidery?
A: Consolidate revisions against the sample; confirm the revised specifications before production.
Q: Can you meet our deadline?
A: Share the required date. Check material availability, sample approval, payment requirements and production capacity before committing.
Q: What if quality or color is wrong?
A: Acknowledge the concern, request photos and the approved reference, then escalate for review. Obtain approval before substitutions.
Q: Can you discount or arrange delivery?
A: Verify current approved pricing, quantity tier, inclusions, dispatch and delivery fees. Never reuse a private negotiated quote.
